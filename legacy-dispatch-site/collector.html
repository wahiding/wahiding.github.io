Section 06 — Collector

Coins, kept for the story, not the value.

A small, ongoing collection — each coin chosen because of where it's been, not what it's worth.

Collects Coins from different countries and eras, valued for their history.

I don't collect for appraisal value. A worn coin from a country I've never visited, carried by hands I'll never know, is more interesting to me than a mint-condition one that's only interesting because of its price tag.

It's a quieter version of the same instinct behind everything else here: notice the detail everyone else skips past, and ask what story it's actually telling.
