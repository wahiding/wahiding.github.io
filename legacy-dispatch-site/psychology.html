Section 05 — Psychology Enthusiast

Every system has people behind it, eventually.

An interest in identity, memory, and self-awareness that started in a personal journal and now shows up in how I think about users, teams, and audiences.

Background Reflective practice via 23 er Diary; reading on identity, memory, and self-awareness.

I'm not a trained psychologist — this is a reader's interest, not a professional one. But years of journaling about identity and memory built a habit of asking why, not just what: why a person reacts the way they do, not just that they did.

That habit turns out to be directly useful at work. A confusing user flow is rarely a technical mystery; it's usually a mismatch between what the system assumes and how a person actually thinks. The same curiosity that makes journaling interesting makes user research interesting.

Focus Areas

Identity & Memory

User Psychology
