Section 02 — Writer

I write to understand, not to impress.

Writing is where I put the QA instinct and the personal instinct in the same room — precise where it needs to be, honest everywhere else.

Long-running project 23 er Diary — a journal kept since 2023, on identity, memory, and the small evidence of change.

A defect report and a diary entry aren't as different as they sound. Both start with an observation, ask what actually happened versus what was supposed to happen, and refuse to move on until the gap is named honestly.

23 er Diary is where that habit runs without a ticket number attached — pages on memory, identity, and the version of myself I was a year ago versus now. It's less a public blog than a working record, the same way test logs are a working record of a system's honesty.

The rest of the writing is more practical: documentation that turns a tangle of architecture decisions into something a new teammate can actually follow, and essays that try to do the same for less technical questions.
