Section 03 — Artist

Mood before precision.

Where QA is about narrowing down to one exact cause, painting is the opposite discipline — staying with something before it resolves into meaning.

Medium Paint and abstraction. Process over polish.

Most of my week is spent making sure a system behaves exactly as specified. Painting is the deliberate exception — no spec, no expected result, just color and instinct until something on the page feels true.

The work leans abstract: mood and color relationships over representation. It's less about finishing a piece than about staying present with an uncertain process, which is a muscle that testing doesn't ask for and painting does.

I don't treat this as a parallel career — it's closer to cross-training. Looking at color and composition long enough sharpens the same attention I bring to a UI review.

Focus Areas

Abstract Painting

Color and mood as the subject, not a supporting detail.

Process Over Product

Sketches and studies valued as much as finished pieces.

Visual Attention

The same close-noticing habit as QA, pointed at a canvas instead of a screen.
