About this dispatch

I'm Wahidul Alam Nahid — a QA engineer by trade, a polymath by instinct. I test payment systems for a living, tracing failures back through logs and pipelines until the cause is undeniable. The same instinct doesn't clock out at 6pm; it just changes subjects, toward words, paint, film, and old coins.

I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from AUST, and currently work on the Platform & Partners team at Ding, an international payments platform, focused on backend and API testing. Outside of work, I keep a long-running journal, 23 er Diary, and shoot short films built more on silence and rhythm than dialogue.

This site isn't a résumé. It's a working notebook, organized the way a magazine organizes its sections — because I've never found one label big enough to hold all of it at once.
